1. Fluffy Buttermilk Pancakes

Prep Time: 10 mins | Cook Time: 15 mins | Servings: 4 | Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients List

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 cups buttermilk
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/4 cup unsalted butter, melted

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Whisk dry ingredients together in a large bowl.
  2. In a separate bowl, whisk wet ingredients.
  3. Pour wet ingredients into dry ingredients; stir gently until just combined.
  4. Heat a lightly oiled griddle and cook until bubbles pop, flip, and brown.

2. Classic Avocado Toast

Prep Time: 5 mins | Cook Time: 2 mins | Servings: 1 | Difficulty: Very Easy

Ingredients List

  • 1 thick slice of sourdough bread
  • 1 ripe Hass avocado
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Red pepper flakes
  • Salt

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Toast your slice of sourdough bread to your desired crispiness.
  2. Mash the avocado with lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  3. Spread the mashed avocado evenly over the warm toasted bread.
  4. Top with a sprinkle of red pepper flakes.

3. Grilled Chicken Caesar Salad

Prep Time: 15 mins | Cook Time: 12 mins | Servings: 2 | Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients List

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 1 head Romaine lettuce
  • 1/2 cup croutons
  • 1/4 cup Parmesan
  • 1/2 cup Caesar dressing

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Brush chicken with olive oil, salt, pepper; grill for 6 mins per side.
  2. Let chicken rest, then slice thinly.
  3. Toss chopped Romaine with Caesar dressing and croutons.
  4. Top with chicken and garnish with shaved Parmesan.

4. Authentic Spaghetti Carbonara

Prep Time: 10 mins | Cook Time: 15 mins | Servings: 2 | Difficulty: Medium-Hard

Media Note: Audio commentary & Video guide included in the digital source code.

Ingredients List

  • 200g Spaghetti
  • 100g Guanciale
  • 4 fresh egg yolks
  • 50g Pecorino Romano
  • Black pepper

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Cook spaghetti until al dente. Fry cubed guanciale until crisp.
  2. Whisk egg yolks and grated Pecorino Romano together into a paste.
  3. Drain pasta, reserve 1/2 cup water. Toss pasta with guanciale fat off heat.
  4. Quickly stir in egg paste and a splash of pasta water until a creamy sauce forms.

5. Garlic Butter Baked Salmon

Prep Time: 10 mins | Cook Time: 15 mins | Servings: 2 | Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients List

  • 2 Salmon fillets
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp dried dill

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Line sheet with foil.
  2. Whisk melted butter, minced garlic, lemon juice, and dill.
  3. Brush salmon fillets with the mixture and bake for 12-15 minutes.

6. Chunky Homemade Guacamole

Prep Time: 10 mins | Cook Time: 0 mins | Servings: 4 | Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients List

  • 3 ripe Hass avocados
  • 1 Roma tomato
  • 1/2 cup onion
  • 1/4 cup cilantro
  • 1 lime juice
  • Salt

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Slice avocados, remove pits, and scoop the flesh out.
  2. Mash avocado gently, leaving plenty of chunky texture.
  3. Fold in the diced tomato, chopped onion, cilantro, and lime juice.

7. Creamy Roasted Garlic Hummus

Prep Time: 15 mins | Cook Time: 0 mins | Servings: 6 | Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients List

  • 1 can chickpeas
  • 1/3 cup tahini
  • 4 cloves roasted garlic
  • 3 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Process tahini and lemon juice until whipped and creamy.
  2. Add olive oil, roasted garlic, cumin, and half the chickpeas; process.
  3. Add remaining chickpeas and process until smooth.

8. Fudgy Chocolate Brownies

Prep Time: 15 mins | Cook Time: 22 mins | Servings: 9 | Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients List

  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1/3 cup cocoa powder
  • 1/2 cup flour

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F and grease an 8x8 inch square baking pan.
  2. Stir melted butter and sugar together until well blended.
  3. Add eggs one at a time, then stir in vanilla extract.
  4. Sift in cocoa, flour, and salt. Bake for 20-22 minutes.
